# Related work and AGP positioning

Reviewed 29 October 2026. AGP was proposed by Marco A. The project combines established practices; this targeted comparison does not establish technical novelty, patentability, or the absence of an identical implementation.

[Microsoft Agentic Behavior Engineering](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/ai-ml/guide/agentic-behavior-engineering), updated 5 October 2026, is the closest methodological overlap. It specifies behavior contracts for bounded situations, required evidence, approvals and escalation, decision records, governed sources, runtime controls, and scenario verification. Consequently, “ABE governs agents while AGP uniquely governs tasks” overstates the distinction. AGP emphasizes a proposed PAL policy, verifier requirements, an explicit claim ledger, and a portable execution receipt. It could complement an ABE implementation.

[NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and its [Generative AI Profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) provide broader risk-management and evaluation guidance. AGP is a small implementation aid within that broader work. Using it provides no NIST certification or evidence of complete framework coverage. Pin the version referenced in an implementation; the NIST overview records that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised.

[NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails) applies programmable controls at input, retrieval, dialogue, execution, and output stages. [Guardrails AI](https://guardrailsai.com/guardrails/docs/api_reference_markdown/types) already supplies validation failure actions such as fix, reask, refrain, and exception. AGP's delivery gate and correction concepts use established mechanisms. Its potential value is selecting required controls consistently for a task and recording their consequences.

[LangGraph interrupts](https://reference.langchain.com/python/langgraph/types/interrupt) support pauses and resumptions for human input. [LangChain's human review implementation](https://github.com/langchain-ai/docs/blob/main/src/oss/langchain/human-in-the-loop.mdx) includes approval, editing, and rejection. An AGP integration could use these tools, while separately ensuring that an authorized person reviewed the exact proposed artifact.

[Promptfoo context faithfulness](https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/configuration/expected-outputs/model-graded/context-faithfulness/) extracts factual claims and checks their support against context. [DeepEval faithfulness](https://github.com/confident-ai/deepeval/blob/main/docs/content/docs/%28rag%29/metrics-faithfulness.mdx) also evaluates output alignment with retrieved context. Claim-level evaluation is therefore established. AGP proposes attaching authority, freshness, locators, and delivery consequences to these findings. Automated judges remain fallible, and support from an incorrect source does not establish truth.

Claim-and-evidence assurance predates LLMs. [OMG SACM](https://www.omg.org/spec/SACM/2.1/PDF) represents auditable claims, arguments, evidence, and their relationships. A flat AGP ledger does not supply all the reasoning of a structured assurance case. [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/) and [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) already model entities, activities, agents, derivations, and responsibility. A future receipt format could map to that vocabulary.

AGP's useful contribution may be accessible packaging: a small policy engine, explicit failure examples, evidence records, and inspectable receipts that fit an existing workflow. That is a product hypothesis and an engineering direction. Demonstrated improvement, adoption, and commercial demand require separate evidence. Recognition should rest on reproducible results and useful maintenance, with credit to the work above.
